About Martian Eye

Autonomous Martian Dust Devil Detection & Intelligence Platform v2.0

Martian Eye is an advanced, autonomous deep-learning platform engineered for continuous atmospheric monitoring on Mars. Built upon a hybrid CNN Backbone (ResNet-34) integrated with a 4-head Multiscale Spatial Attention Transformer, the platform detects faint, transient vortex plumes rising against complex terrain.

To guarantee high reliability during surface rover operations, Martian Eye features a Stage-2 Hard Negative Verifier to filter non-plume terrain false positives (crater rims, dune ripples, mesa shadows), backed by Grad-CAM Explainability (XAI) and Cross-Mission Domain Generalization across rover surface and orbital satellite imaging.

The pipeline provides automated morphology profiling (column height, pixel width, aspect ratio, orientation angle), epistemic uncertainty risk bands, and instant telemetry export for rover mission planners.
